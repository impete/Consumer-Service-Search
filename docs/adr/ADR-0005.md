# ADR-0005: Compliance baseline

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-04

## Context
The app is public-facing and handles location inputs.

## Decision
Adopt GDPR/CCPA readiness, WCAG 2.1 AA, and OAuth-based auth as the baseline.

## Consequences
Positive: trust and lower legal risk. Negative: extra engineering effort.
